# AdSense production plan

The site is AdSense-ready but does not contain a fake publisher ID.

Recommended placements:
1. One responsive ad area between the hero and long-form explanation on tool pages.
2. One content-area ad on category/list pages where it does not interfere with navigation.
3. Optional in-content ad after useful explanatory content.

Do not place ads immediately beside upload, convert, download, navigation or test buttons in a way that could cause accidental clicks. Do not label ads as navigation or helpful links.

Before applying for AdSense, replace placeholder legal/contact text and make sure the site has original, useful explanatory content around the tools.
